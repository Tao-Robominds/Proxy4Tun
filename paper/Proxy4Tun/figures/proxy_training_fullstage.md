# proxy_training_fullstage

**Figure.** Training fit of the full-stage proxy on 40 trials × 3 families (n = 120). Scatter points show lean \(p(e{+}c)\) predictions versus ground-truth mIoU, coloured by tunnel family. Trend lines compare feature ablations: evidence-only \(p(e)\), coherence-only \(p(c)\), full \(p(e{+}c)\), and the selected lean \(p(e{+}c)\) (solid). Reported MAE = 0.129 and Spearman = 0.853 are for lean \(p(e{+}c)\).
