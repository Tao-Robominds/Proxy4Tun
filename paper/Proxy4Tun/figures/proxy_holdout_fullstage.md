# proxy_holdout_fullstage

**Figure.** Holdout evaluation of the full-stage proxy on 9 subsets × 3 families (n = 54; 27 known-good anchors and 27 known-bad configs). Scatter points show unified lean \(p(e{+}c)\) predictions versus ground-truth mIoU, coloured by tunnel family (circles: known-good; crosses: known-bad). The red dashed line marks the alarm threshold at 0.5. Trend lines compare the unified lean \(p(e{+}c)\) (solid) with per-family lean refits (dotted). Proxy scores are affine-scaled into \([0, 1]\) for display.
